`invoiceTotals` in `src/invoice.js` worked out VAT for each line, rounded it, then added the rounded amounts. That broke the README rule to round once at the end, so the GB test invoice (three 3-cent lines) came to 3 cents of VAT when 20% of the 9-cent net is 1.8, which rounds to 2. It now charges VAT once on the invoice's total net with `vatOn(net, region)`, and `npm test` passes all 29 tests.
