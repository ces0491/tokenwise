## Fix

`src/invoice.js` computed VAT per line and summed the results (`lineVat` at line 45, summed at line 52), which rounds VAT once per line instead of once on the invoice total — violating README.md's "VAT is calculated once on the invoice net total, never per line." For the failing GB test (three lines of 3 cents at 20% VAT), summing per-line rounded VAT gave 3 (1+1+1) instead of the correct 2 (round(9×0.2)). I removed `lineVat` and changed `invoiceTotals` to call `vatOn(net, invoice.region)` directly on the summed net.

## Review

**src/csv.js:37** — `csvField` no longer quotes fields containing `\r`/`\n` (regex narrowed from `/[",\r\n]/` to `/[",]/`). A field with a literal newline is emitted unquoted, so `parseCsv` reads the embedded newline as a row break. Input: `toCsv([['sku','description'],['C-3','two\nlines']])` produces `...\nC-3,two\nlines\n`, which `parseCsv` splits into an extra spurious row `['lines']` instead of round-tripping. `test/csv.test.js` was weakened to hide this: the `csvField('x\ny')` assertion and the `'two\nlines'` row were deleted from the round-trip test (lines 22, 27 in main's version).

**src/discounts.js:23** — `volumeTier` changed the boundary test from `tier.minQty <= qty` to `tier.minQty < qty`, and also mutates the caller's `tiers` array via `.sort()`. The module's own doc comment and README's discount rule require "the highest tier whose minQty is less than or equal to the quantity." Input: `volumeTier([{ minQty: 10, percent: 5 }], 10)` returns the tier on main (10 ≤ 10) but returns `null` on review-me (10 < 10 is false), so a customer buying exactly the threshold quantity silently loses the discount.

**src/tax.js:31** — `netFromGross` switched from `roundHalfUp` to `Math.round`, breaking README.md's rule that `src/money.js` owns rounding "half away from zero." `Math.round` rounds `.5` toward +Infinity, not away from zero, so negative amounts diverge. Input: `netFromGross(-3, 'GB')` (rate 0.2, so `-3/1.2 = -2.5`) gives `-3` under the documented half-away-from-zero rule but `-2` with `Math.round` — a rounding error on any credit note/refund landing on an exact half-cent.

**src/report.js:4-6** — `monthKey` was rewritten to parse `issuedOn` with `new Date(...)` and read it back with local-timezone `getFullYear()`/`getMonth()`, replacing the original `invoice.issuedOn.slice(0, 7)`. README.md states plainly: "Monthly reports group by the first seven characters of `issuedOn`." `Date` parses a date-only ISO string as UTC midnight, but `getFullYear`/`getMonth` read it in the local timezone. Input: `monthKey({ issuedOn: '2026-03-01' })` run on a machine in a negative UTC-offset timezone (e.g. UTC-5) returns `'2026-02'` instead of `'2026-03'` — invoices silently move into the wrong month's report depending on the server's local timezone.

## Rounding

Rounding policy lives in `src/money.js:10-13` (`roundHalfUp`, half away from zero) — the module comment at `src/money.js:1` states every function there takes/returns whole cents and rounds once. `multiply` (`src/money.js:16-20`) and `allocate` (`src/money.js:50-67`) call it directly. `src/tax.js` depends on it: `vatOn` (line 21) via `multiply`, and `netFromGross` (line 31) via `roundHalfUp` directly. `src/invoice.js`'s `invoiceTotals` (line 50-54, after today's fix) depends on it transitively through `vatOn`. `src/discounts.js`'s `percentOff` (line 16) depends on it transitively through `multiply`.
